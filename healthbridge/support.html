FAQ

Where are my exported files?
In the Files app → On My iPhone → HealthBridge. You can share them or move them anywhere yourself.

Does HealthBridge send my data anywhere?
No. It is read-only and fully offline. Data leaves your device only when you explicitly share or move a file.

What does HealthBridge Pro unlock?
Date-range and last-N-days export, the full Shortcuts automation suite, and AI-ready sharing. It is a one-time purchase. Use "Restore Purchases" on the paywall or in Settings if you switch devices.

Shortcuts export asks for parameters?
Use "Export Last N Days" (asks for a number) or "Export Custom Range" (asks for start/end dates). "Export Today" needs no input.

常见问题

导出的文件在哪?
「文件」App →「我的 iPhone」→ HealthBridge。你可以自行分享或移动到任意位置。

HealthBridge 会上传我的数据吗?
不会。它是只读且完全离线的;只有你主动分享或移动文件时,数据才会离开设备。

HealthBridge Pro 解锁什么?
日期范围/最近 N 天导出、快捷指令自动化全家桶、AI 就绪分享。一次买断;换设备用付费墙或设置里的「恢复购买」。

快捷指令导出会询问参数吗?
「导出最近 N 天」会问天数,「导出自定义范围」会问起止日期,「导出今天」无需输入。
