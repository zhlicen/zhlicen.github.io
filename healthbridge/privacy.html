HealthBridge Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 11, 2026

Summary

HealthBridge does not collect, transmit, or sell any of your data. Everything stays on your device.

Health Data

HealthBridge reads data from Apple Health (HealthKit) with your explicit permission, solely to generate export files (JSON/Markdown) that are saved locally on your device. HealthBridge is read-only: it never writes to or modifies your Apple Health data.

No Collection, No Servers

HealthBridge has no server component, requires no account, and makes no network requests. We do not collect personal information, analytics, usage data, or crash reports. Your health data is never used for advertising or shared with third parties.

Sharing Is Under Your Control

Exported files leave your device only when you explicitly choose to share or move them (e.g., via the system Share Sheet, the Files app, Shortcuts automations you create, or your own iCloud Drive). Any such transfer is initiated and controlled entirely by you and is governed by the terms of the destination you choose.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this page.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/zhlicen/HealthBridge.

HealthBridge 隐私政策

最后更新:2026 年 7 月 11 日

摘要

HealthBridge 不收集、不上传、不出售你的任何数据。一切都留在你的设备上。

健康数据

HealthBridge 在你明确授权后读取 Apple「健康」(HealthKit) 数据,仅用于在你的设备本地生成导出文件(JSON/Markdown)。HealthBridge 是只读的:绝不写入或修改你的健康数据。

零收集、零服务器

HealthBridge 没有服务器组件,无需账号,不发起任何网络请求。我们不收集个人信息、分析数据、使用数据或崩溃报告。你的健康数据绝不会被用于广告或分享给第三方。

分享由你掌控

导出文件只有在你主动选择分享或移动时才会离开设备(例如系统分享面板、「文件」App、你自建的快捷指令自动化,或你自己的 iCloud Drive)。任何此类传输均由你发起和控制,并受你所选目的地条款约束。

变更

政策如有变更,将更新于本页面。
